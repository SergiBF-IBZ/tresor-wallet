The client never signs blind. The review screen is a plain-language simulation: who gets what, the balance after, who pays the fee. Swissquote's screening runs before Face ID and is shown: sanctions, verified counterparties such as the client's own Swissquote account, a warning on a first payment to a new address (the classic address-swap scam), and the limits. Then the same rules are enforced again on Swissquote's side when co-signing, so a tampered app gains nothing. In the demo we show the sanctioned entity flagged, then refused.

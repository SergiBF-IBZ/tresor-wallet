Closer to software than to custody

Custody

Swissquote holds 1 of 3 shares and cannot act alone; no partner holds a share. Blocking a payment is not moving funds.

Travel Rule

A BIP-322 ownership proof at creation and after every recovery. Account-to-own-wallet transfers are verified automatically.

Paying third parties

The client initiates and controls; Swissquote co-signs and screens. We argue it is not the sending institution, and screen anyway.

Simplified reasoning, not legal advice. The grey zone the challenge names is flagged, not hidden: if FINMA disagreed, Swissquote already holds what it would need (owner records, screening, sanctions refusals).

Custody depends on who can move the funds. Swissquote cannot move them alone, so this is software with a co-signing service, not a deposit; no capital or segregation rules for these assets. The Travel Rule needs proven ownership of the self-hosted wallet before a Swiss bank sends or receives: our ownership proof makes that check automatic, and the proof is renewed after every recovery. Third-party payments: the client decides, signs and broadcasts; Swissquote only adds a co-signature after screening, like a 2FA provider with compliance checks. That is our argument for "not the sending institution". If a regulator saw it differently, the data needed is already collected.

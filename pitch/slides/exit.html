5 · If Swissquote disappears

The funds were never at Swissquote

1 · Export

The app exports Share 1 offline, behind Face ID. No server needed.

2 · Card + PIN

The recovery card releases Share 3 after its PIN check.

3 · Sign and send

The independent Exit Tool signs locally and broadcasts via a public explorer.

Open source, not Swissquote-branded, never contacts the co-signer. A Swissquote freeze cannot stop it, which is exactly what makes the wallet non-custodial.

The challenge asks how the client gets their money back if Swissquote is permanently gone. Answer: the coins are on the blockchain, not at Swissquote; only the app and servers disappear. Phone plus card are two shares, enough to sign without anyone. We built the Exit Tool as a separate program with its own look, and it has no way to reach the co-signer. We ran it with real funds. Honest note: in production the export must be possible before the app disappears from the stores, so it is designed from day one and works offline.

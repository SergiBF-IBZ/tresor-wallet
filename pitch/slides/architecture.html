Every box is its own process with its own storage, as the challenge requires: the co-signer runs separately, and the phone app never sees Share 2, it only exchanges MPC protocol messages. The co-signer rebuilds what it signs from the transaction itself, so it never signs a hash it did not check. Real: MPC, the co-signer and its policy, fee sponsoring, BIP-322 proofs, the encrypted backup, the Exit Tool, the signet transactions. Mocked and labelled: the card hardware, Face ID, video identification, the Apple/Google account, the sanctions provider.

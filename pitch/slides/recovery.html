The rule behind the table: if a share may be in someone else's hands, move the funds to a brand-new wallet; if a share was only lost, re-share the same key. A stolen phone means the thief keeps that share forever, so we sweep to a new wallet with new shares. A lost card is only lost, so phone and Swissquote re-share the same key onto a new card; the old card share no longer combines (we test that). Phone and card both lost: the Apple/Google backup, double-locked with Swissquote's key, with a 7-day wait because a thief with an unlocked phone may also hold the passkey. The bonus scenario, thief with phone and email, is refused twice: at video ID, and at the sweep, because in recovery Swissquote only pairs with the card. All of these ran on signet.

08

Where keys are generated: in pieces, on two machines. The phone runs the phone and card parties; Swissquote runs its own party on its server, so its share never exists anywhere else. Identity link: the wallet is created inside the logged-in Swissquote session, and then signs a BIP-322 ownership message. Swissquote writes the message and computes what is signed itself, so this route can never be abused to sign a payment. Because Swissquote cannot sign alone, a valid signature proves the client took part. After a recovery, the new wallet proves ownership again automatically.

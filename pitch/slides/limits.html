What is simulated, and what comes next

Simulated today, labelled DEMO

  • Recovery card hardware (FIDO2 NFC card)
  • Face ID and video identification
  • Apple/Google passkey and cloud drive
  • Sanctions provider, counterparty directory
  • Client login: a token instead of a passkey

Known limits, next steps

  • Two shares briefly on one device at creation, recovery and exit: wiped at once
  • MPC library licence is non-commercial
  • Fee wallet moves to an HSM-backed service
  • Taproot design: on-chain timelocks for the exit
  • EVM: USDC paymaster and approved-dApp list
  • Open source, reproducible builds, audit

Say the limits before the judges do. Everything simulated is a component a bank buys or already has: FIDO2 cards, video identification providers, sanctions screening, passkey login. The real engineering risk is the moment one device holds two shares; we keep it short and on a fresh device, and the production card would seal its share immediately. The MPC library needs a commercial licence for launch. The Taproot design in WORKFLOW section 15 adds on-chain timelocks so the exit no longer depends on any software.

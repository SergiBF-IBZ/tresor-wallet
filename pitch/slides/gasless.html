The challenge allows "the equivalent on your chain". On Bitcoin the client's fee comes out of their own coins; we remove that. Swissquote's fee wallet, its own money, adds one coin to the client's payment and that coin pays the whole fee. Who pays: Swissquote, covered by the premium tier and trading revenue. How abuse is prevented: only verified clients, Swissquote signs last and only for a payment it already approved under the normal policy, hard caps per payment and per day, and each coin is used once. Swissquote's signature only commits its own coin, so it can never lose more than the capped fee. Live transaction on screen.

Swissquote can block, never move

Can Swissquote move the funds alone?

No. It holds 1 of 3 shares, and one share cannot sign (automated test). Its fee wallet only adds Swissquote's own coins.

Swissquote together with a partner?

No partner holds a share. The optional cloud backup needs Apple/Google and Swissquote together: disclosed, and skippable.

Who could steal the funds?

Someone with two of: the unlocked phone, the card and its PIN, Swissquote's co-signature (screened and limited).

Who could lock the client out?

Only losing phone and card with no backup. A Swissquote freeze never does: phone and card can always leave.

These are the challenge's four questions, answered directly. The key line for the legal discussion: Swissquote alone, or with any partner, cannot move funds. The one honest caveat is the optional backup: Apple or Google plus Swissquote together could open it, for example under a court order. That is the same exposure as any wallet on their phones, it is disclosed, and the client can skip it. Freezing is Swissquote's power; locking the client out is not, because phone plus card always works.

02

Today a Swissquote client who wants "not your keys, not your coins" has one option: withdraw to an external wallet. Swissquote loses the assets and the trading, and under FINMA's Travel Rule it cannot even verify who owns that wallet. The client gets 24 words to protect alone. We want a third option: self-custody that stays one step from the account.

Our answer

One key, split in three. Any two sign.

Share 1 · Your phone

Behind Face ID. Signs everyday payments together with Swissquote.

Share 2 · Swissquote

An isolated co-signer. Screens sanctions and limits. Can block, never move.

Share 3 · Recovery card

NFC card with a PIN, kept at home. For large payments, recovery and the exit.

The full key never exists anywhere. No seed words to lose. Swissquote pays the network fees.

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

03

MPC threshold signatures, DKLs23, an established open-source library: no homemade crypto. The key is generated in pieces and never put together, not even to sign. Phone plus Swissquote is daily use. Card plus Swissquote is recovery. Phone plus card is the exit if Swissquote is gone. Swissquote alone holds one share: it can refuse to sign, it can never sign alone. Same key works for Bitcoin and EVM chains.

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

05

We judged every model on one question first: who can move the money? Custodial means Swissquote can, which is bank custody and gives the client nothing new. A seed phrase puts everything on 24 words a typical client will photograph or lose. ERC-4337 is elegant but EVM-only, and Swissquote clients hold bitcoin. Shamir splits the seed but must rebuild it to sign, and Swissquote plus a partner could rebuild it without the client. MPC 2-of-3 gives multi-chain, no seed, and Swissquote can only ever block. The cost: a library licence and no on-chain timelocks with a single key, which is why the production design adds Taproot.

1 · Threat model

Swissquote can block, never move

Can Swissquote move the funds alone?

No. It holds 1 of 3 shares, and one share cannot sign (automated test). Its fee wallet only adds Swissquote's own coins.

Swissquote together with a partner?

No partner holds a share. The optional cloud backup needs Apple/Google and Swissquote together: disclosed, and skippable.

Who could steal the funds?

Someone with two of: the unlocked phone, the card and its PIN, Swissquote's co-signature (screened and limited).

Who could lock the client out?

Only losing phone and card with no backup. A Swissquote freeze never does: phone and card can always leave.

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

06

These are the challenge's four questions, answered directly. The key line for the legal discussion: Swissquote alone, or with any partner, cannot move funds. The one honest caveat is the optional backup: Apple or Google plus Swissquote together could open it, for example under a court order. That is the same exposure as any wallet on their phones, it is disclosed, and the client can skip it. Freezing is Swissquote's power; locking the client out is not, because phone plus card always works.

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

08

Where keys are generated: in pieces, on two machines. The phone runs the phone and card parties; Swissquote runs its own party on its server, so its share never exists anywhere else. Identity link: the wallet is created inside the logged-in Swissquote session, and then signs a BIP-322 ownership message. Swissquote writes the message and computes what is signed itself, so this route can never be abused to sign a payment. Because Swissquote cannot sign alone, a valid signature proves the client took part. After a recovery, the new wallet proves ownership again automatically.

Daily limit. Lowering is instant; raising needs the card.

Before every signature, in plain language: who receives what, who pays the fee, sanctions and limit checks, the balance after.

A thief with an unlocked phone cannot enrol their own card: card keys are registered only at creation, recovery or a verified replacement.

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

09

Daily use is Face ID plus the co-signer session (passkeys in production; a token in the prototype). Swissquote enforces the tiers on its side, so a modified app cannot skip them. The card tap is a FIDO2-style signature with the key registered at wallet creation; the challenge is single-use and bound to the transaction id, so it cannot be replayed for another payment. Limits default to 25,000 and 100,000 sats in the demo; clients change them in Settings, and raising them needs the card.

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

10

The rule behind the table: if a share may be in someone else's hands, move the funds to a brand-new wallet; if a share was only lost, re-share the same key. A stolen phone means the thief keeps that share forever, so we sweep to a new wallet with new shares. A lost card is only lost, so phone and Swissquote re-share the same key onto a new card; the old card share no longer combines (we test that). Phone and card both lost: the Apple/Google backup, double-locked with Swissquote's key, with a 7-day wait because a thief with an unlocked phone may also hold the passkey. The bonus scenario, thief with phone and email, is refused twice: at video ID, and at the sweep, because in recovery Swissquote only pairs with the card. All of these ran on signet.

The challenge asks how the client gets their money back if Swissquote is permanently gone. Answer: the coins are on the blockchain, not at Swissquote; only the app and servers disappear. Phone plus card are two shares, enough to sign without anyone. We built the Exit Tool as a separate program with its own look, and it has no way to reach the co-signer. We ran it with real funds. The challenge allows "the equivalent on your chain". On Bitcoin the client's fee comes out of their own coins; we remove that. Swissquote's fee wallet, its own money, adds one coin to the client's payment and that coin pays the whole fee. Who pays: Swissquote, covered by the premium tier and trading revenue. Legal feasibility · Switzerland

Closer to software than to custody

Custody

Swissquote holds 1 of 3 shares and cannot act alone; no partner holds a share. Blocking a payment is not moving funds.

Travel Rule

A BIP-322 ownership proof at creation and after every recovery. Account-to-own-wallet transfers are verified automatically.

Paying third parties

The client initiates and controls; Swissquote co-signs and screens. We argue it is not the sending institution, and screen anyway.

Simplified reasoning, not legal advice. The grey zone the challenge names is flagged, not hidden: if FINMA disagreed, Swissquote already holds what it would need (owner records, screening, sanctions refusals).

Swissquote Wallet · EPFL Hackathon 2026

14

Custody depends on who can move the funds. Swissquote cannot move them alone, so this is software with a co-signing service, not a deposit; no capital or segregation rules for these assets. The Travel Rule needs proven ownership of the self-hosted wallet before a Swiss bank sends or receives: our ownership proof makes that check automatic, and the proof is renewed after every recovery. Third-party payments: the client decides, signs and broadcasts; Swissquote only adds a co-signature after screening, like a 2FA provider with compliance checks. That is our argument for "not the sending institution". If a regulator saw it differently, the data needed is already collected.
